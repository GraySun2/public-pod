# Library

The research library: market, concept, player, craft and operations research, and nothing else.
To start, read [INDEX.md](INDEX.md): one row per item, by shelf and date.
To add something, read [FILING.md](FILING.md). Agents also read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).

The items here when the repo started are research shared from the original Pod, an indie game studio's. They are written for a small team with AI agents doing much of the build, shipping on Steam PC or phone web first. Each item says what it rests on in its `basis:` line; read that before trusting a number. Where an item mentions "the owner" or "the studio", it means the studio the research was first done for.

In your own private Pod, `players/` will hold real study answers: anyone with access to the repo can read them, so keep it to study IDs.
