# Library

The research library: market, concept, player, craft and operations research, and nothing else.
To start, read [INDEX.md](INDEX.md): one row per item, by shelf and date.
To add something, read [FILING.md](FILING.md). Agents also read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).

The Library starts with three briefs on context engineering, shared from the original Pod: the field overview, second-brain designs, and the Interpretable Context Methodology. The shelves for market, concept, player and craft research are there for your own. Each item says what it rests on in its `basis:` line; read that before trusting a number. Where an item mentions "the owner" or "the studio", it means the studio the research was first done for.

In your own private Pod, `players/` will hold real study answers: anyone with access to the repo can read them, so keep it to study IDs.
