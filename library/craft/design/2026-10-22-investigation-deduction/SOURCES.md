## Evidence limits

- S2, S6, S8 and S10 are Wikipedia summaries; their primary sources (GUMSHOE rulebooks, Knox's essay, the Kotaku review, the games) were not read.
- S12 reports interviews second-hand. For S15 only the abstract was read.
- S13 and S14 come from escape rooms and pen-and-paper logic puzzles, not digital mystery games.
- No source was paywalled; no paywalled text is used.
- Not used for any claim (seen but not read): Game Maker's Toolkit videos "What Makes a Good Detective Game?" (2017) and "The 3 Types of Detective Game" (2023-08-11), Mark Brown.
