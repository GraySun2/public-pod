## Evidence limits

- S2, S4, S8, S17, S20 and S21 are secondary. The primary sources behind them (Faulkner's paper, the RITE paper, Cronin's slides, tinyBuild's post, the GameAnalytics PDF, the book) were not read.
- For S14, only a summary page was read, not the paper.
- S12, S13 and S20 are vendor material or sponsored, and S18 and S19 rely on self-reported surveys of developers who chose to share numbers.
- S1, S2, S3 and S5 come from software usability, not games; their use for playtests is our inference except where S6 makes the same point.
- No paywalled text is used.
