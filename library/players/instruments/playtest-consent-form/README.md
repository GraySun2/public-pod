# Playtest consent form (template)

A shared consent form for any Library project's playtests, in plain words. It says who runs the test, what is recorded, how long it is kept and who sees it, that the tester is known by an MRP ID only, and that they can stop at any time and ask for deletion. Clips in public material are a separate, optional yes. A remote variant is read aloud and recorded at the start of the session. No responses are recorded in this library.

**How to use it.** Fill in every `{curly-bracket}` placeholder, give the form a version number, and send it before the session. It follows the consent and data-minimisation notes in [the playtesting study](../../../craft/design/2026-10-22-playtesting-and-player-research/README.md), section 3, and the Library's ID-only rule ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md) rule 3). Pair it with the [playtest screener](../playtest-screener/README.md).

**What the Library keeps.** Only the consent record: MRP ID, form version, date (no time or time zone) and the yes or no answers in part 7. A signed copy, the tester's name and their contact details stay outside the Library, held by one person at the studio.

## Playtest consent form

Form version: `{version}` · Game: `{game name or code name}` · Session date: `{YYYY-MM-DD}`

### 1. Who we are and what this is

`{Studio name}` is making a game that is not out yet. We would like you to play it for about `{length, for example 45-60 minutes}` while we watch and take notes. We are testing the game, not you. There are no wrong answers, and anything that confuses you is useful to us.

The session is `{in person at place / online over voice or video call}`, with `{number}` other players. `{Facilitator role, for example "One person from the studio"}` will talk you through it, and up to `{number}` others will watch quietly.

### 2. What we record

- **Your screen** while you play, `{and the game's own logs, such as how long a level takes}`.
- **Your voice**, including what you say to other players.
- **Your camera only if the test needs it** (`{reason, or "not used in this test"}`). We will ask first, and you can say no.
- **Notes** written by the people watching.

### 3. Who sees it and how long we keep it

- Only `{Studio name}`'s team sees the recordings and notes. We don't sell or share them.
- We keep recordings for `{retention period, for example until the end of this test round plus 90 days}`, then delete them. Notes and summaries without your voice or image may be kept longer.
- When we write up results, we quote what you said in our own words, not word for word, unless you say yes in part 7.

### 4. How we know you

You get a study ID (an MRP ID, in the form MRP-NNN). Our notes and write-ups use that ID only. We don't keep your username or handle, links to your accounts, or your time zone in our study notes. The one link between your ID and how to contact you is kept by one person at `{Studio name}` and is deleted when the study ends, unless you say yes to being contacted again.

### 5. You can stop

- Taking part is your choice. You can skip a question, take a break or stop at any time, without giving a reason.
- After the session, you can ask us to delete your recordings and notes. Contact `{contact method kept outside the Library}` and quote your MRP ID.

### 6. Keep the game private

This game is unreleased. Please don't share what you see, take screenshots or record it yourself, or describe it in public until `{Studio name}` says you can. *(To be replaced by the playtest NDA once that exists.)*

### 7. Your choices

Tick yes or no for each. Only the first is needed to take part.

| | Yes | No |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I agree to take part, and to my screen and voice being recorded as in part 2. | ☐ | ☐ |
| You may quote what I said word for word, under my MRP ID. | ☐ | ☐ |
| You may contact me about future tests. | ☐ | ☐ |
| *Optional, separate:* you may use clips of my screen or voice in public material, such as a trailer or a devlog. My name and handle will not be shown. | ☐ | ☐ |

Saying no to the last three does not change anything about the test.

### 8. Age

You must be 21 or over to take part.

☐ I am 21 or over.

### 9. Signature

MRP ID: `{MRP-NNN}` · Date: `{YYYY-MM-DD}` · Form version: `{version}`

## Remote-session variant: consent read aloud

For online sessions, the facilitator reads this at the very start, once recording is on, and the tester answers each question aloud. The recording of this part is the consent record. Note the MRP ID, the date, the form version and each answer.

> Before we start: this is a test of `{game name}` by `{Studio name}`, and it takes about `{length}`. We are recording your screen and your voice, `{and your camera, because reason / but not your camera}`. Only our team sees the recording, and we delete it after `{retention period}`. In our notes you are `{MRP-NNN}`, not your name or handle. You can stop at any time, and you can ask us later to delete your recording. Please don't share or record anything about the game, because it isn't out yet. You need to be 21 or over.
>
> 1. Are you 21 or over?
> 2. Do you agree to take part and to us recording your screen and voice?
> 3. May we quote what you say word for word, under your study ID? Yes or no is fine.
> 4. May we contact you about future tests?
> 5. Separately, and only if you want: may we use clips of your screen or voice in public material, such as a trailer? We would not show your name or handle.
>
> Thank you. Let's start.

If the tester says no to question 1 or 2, stop the recording, thank them and end the session.
