# Playtest screener (template)

A shared screener for picking playtesters for any Library project. Twelve short questions ask how people play, not who they are: co-op habits, genres, recent games, platform and input, whether they have seen the game, their link to the team, group availability and accessibility needs. A scoring note says who to invite for a first-time-user test. No responses are recorded in this library.

**How to use it.** Fill in the `{curly-bracket}` placeholders and trim questions the test does not need. The playtesting study says to screen on behaviour, not demographics, and to collect only what the test needs ([the playtesting study](../../../craft/design/2026-10-22-playtesting-and-player-research/README.md), section 3). Invited testers then get the [playtest consent form](../playtest-consent-form/README.md).

**What the Library keeps.** Answers are filed under the MRP ID the studio assigns, never under a name or handle ([CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md) rule 3). Contact details are asked for on a separate page that stays outside the Library, held by one person, and are deleted when the study ends unless the person agrees to be contacted again. The screener asks for no name, exact age, location, time zone, account names or account links.

## Playtest screener

`{Studio name}` is looking for people to try an unreleased game, `{game name or "a co-op game"}`, for about `{length}`. These questions help us pick a good mix of players. They take about 3 minutes. Skip any you would rather not answer.

**1. Are you 21 or over?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No (we can only invite people aged 21 or over)

**2. How often do you play games with other people (co-op, online or on the same screen)?**

- ☐ Most weeks
- ☐ A few times a month
- ☐ Rarely
- ☐ Never

**3. Who do you usually play co-op games with?** *(tick all that apply)*

- ☐ Friends or family I play with regularly
- ☐ Online friends
- ☐ Strangers, through matchmaking

**4. Which kinds of games have you played in the last month?** *(tick all that apply)*

- ☐ Co-op survival or crafting
- ☐ Puzzle or escape room
- ☐ Action or shooter
- ☐ Strategy or tactics
- ☐ Story or adventure
- ☐ Simulation or management
- ☐ Party games
- ☐ Other: `________`

**5. Name up to three games you played in the last month, and roughly how long you played each.**

| Game | Under 2 hours | 2-10 hours | Over 10 hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ________ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| ________ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| ________ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

**6. What will you play on?**

- ☐ Windows PC
- ☐ Steam Deck
- ☐ Mac
- ☐ Linux PC
- ☐ None of these
- `{Add the build's minimum requirements here if they matter.}`

**7. How do you like to control games like this?**

- ☐ Keyboard and mouse
- ☐ Controller
- ☐ Either is fine

**8. Can you use a headset with a microphone, somewhere quiet, for the session?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ Yes, but I'd rather not talk much
- ☐ No

**9. Have you seen or played `{game name}` before?**

- ☐ Never heard of it
- ☐ Heard of it, but haven't seen it played

**10. Do you know anyone on the `{Studio name}` team?**

- ☐ No
- ☐ I follow the studio online (for example its Discord or Steam page)
- ☐ I've worked with someone on the team
- ☐ A friend or family member is on the team

**11. For a group session of 2-4 players at a set time:** *(tick all that apply)*

- ☐ I could join a group of strangers
- ☐ I could bring 1-3 people I already play with

**12. Is there anything that would make the session easier for you?** *(optional)*

- ☐ Subtitles or captions
- ☐ Colour-blind settings
- ☐ Remapped controls
- ☐ Breaks during the session
- ☐ Something else: `________`

## Scoring note: who to invite for a first-time-user test

A first-time-user test asks whether a stranger can start and understand the game without help ([the playtesting study](../../../craft/design/2026-10-22-playtesting-and-player-research/README.md), sections 1, 4 and 5). Write the player type and the test's pass, revise and stop rule before you screen.

1. **Must have:** Q1 is yes (21 or over; no exceptions); Q6 and Q7 match the build; for a co-op test, Q8 is yes (either answer) and Q11 is not "I can't do a group session".
2. **First-time players only:** Q9 is "Never heard of it" or "Heard of it, but haven't seen it played". "Seen screenshots, videos or streams" is a fallback only. "Played a build" or "I have worked on it" are never invited to a first-time test.
3. **Friends and family are flagged:** Q10 "A friend or family member is on the team" or "I've worked with someone on the team" can join smoke tests (does it launch, can a stranger be talked through it), but they never count toward a gate. "I follow the studio online" is fine.
4. **Match the player type on behaviour:** use Q2-Q5 to place people. For a co-op game, prefer Q2 "Most weeks" or "A few times a month" and recent games in Q4-Q5 that resemble the one under test.
5. **How many:** about 5 first-time players per player type per round, and new players each round. For co-op, 5 groups make a round; count each group as one session. Mix pre-formed groups (Q11 "bring 1-3 people") and matched strangers, because they play differently.
6. **Accessibility:** Q12 never rules anyone out; use it to set up the session.

Give each invited person the next MRP ID before the session, then send the consent form. Keep the screener answers under that ID; keep the contact page outside the Library.
