## Where time leaks

_The repeat jobs and lost time the Pod should fix. In the original Pod these were: two accounts with two memories, long builds without updated estimates, babysitting builds, repos not attached to their projects, and first-time setup on a new computer._

## The plan

1. **Standing instructions** in every session: [instructions.md](instructions.md), pasted from [paste-block.md](paste-block.md).
2. **Expert roster**: [experts.md](experts.md), with each seat unproven until it does good work here.
3. **The Pod** as shared memory across accounts and projects: this repo.
4. **Skills** for the repeat jobs, once a job has come up three times.
5. **Checking the Pod works**: the `read:` and `applied:` lists on history entries, the **Owner corrected** line, and the monthly [quiz](quiz.md).
